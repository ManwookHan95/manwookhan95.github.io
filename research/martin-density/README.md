# Norm-attaining operators into l_2^2 on Martin norms

- `martin_density_note.tex` / `.pdf` (Part I, 78 pages): "Recovery of mates on Martin norms and the resonance obstruction".
  Every theorem in it was checked by an independent adversarial referee pass.
- `martin_density_note_II.tex` / `.pdf` (Part II, 144 pages, draft v1): "Recovery of mates on Martin norms II: companions,
  clean sub-windows and a designed operator". Written from the refereed working notes of rounds 5-8; checked by three
  section referees (39 required fixes, all resolved, see `working-notes/paper2/`). A second referee pass on the revision
  is pending. Results of rounds 9-10 (status coherence for generic weights, mixed classes) are not yet included.
- The density problem itself remains open for every admissible operator (see Part I, Section 9, and Part II, Section 7).
- `working-notes/`: briefing files, Martin's paper summary, and the refereed working notes of rounds 1-9
  (`r1/` ... `r9/`), with referee reports and numerical scripts.
