# Z6 part 8: Farkas pinning of swallowing-type peaks (d-rigidity), and comparison with Z4

Setting of Theorem U (5.3) and of Z4 Theorem A / Corollary 4.1: level l_*, B_* := bad carriers <= l_*, T_0 := union_{B_*} supp y_l,
L_j(nu) := sum_{l in B_*} eps_l nu_l u_l(j), q_l := eps_l Phi_m(k) w_m(k)/(m C_m) for EVERY bad carrier l = j(k,m) (for a peak:
q_l = varsigma eps Phi M/(mC)), Q_m(nu) := sum_{l in B_*, m(l)=m} q_l nu_l.

Definition (d-neutral zero-cost cone with peaks free).  C_0^+(l_*) := {nu in R^{B_*} : nu_l >= 0 (all l in B_*); z_j L_j(nu) >= 0
(j in T_0 \ F, |z_j| = 1); L_j(nu) = 0 (j in T_0 \ F, |z_j| < 1); nu_l = 0 (anti-sign peaks, varsigma eps = -1); Q_m(nu) = 0 (all m)}.
A bad swallowing-type peak p (varsigma eps = +1) is d-RIGID at level l_* if nu_p = 0 for all nu in C_0^+(l_*).
Monotonicity [PROVED]: extension by zero maps C_0^+(l) into C_0^+(l+1) (new rows at j in T_0(l+1) \ T_0(l), j notin F: L_j(nu) is
eps_l nu_l v_l(j) for the unique l with j in S_l, or 0; j is then a contact with z_j = eps_l (swallowed), so the row holds as nu_l >= 0;
the d-rows are linear).  Hence rigidity at level l+1 implies rigidity at level l; "rigid at infinitely many levels" = "rigid at all levels".

Proposition P (Farkas pinning) [PROVED].  If p is d-rigid at level l_*, there is a certificate (y >= 0 on the sign rows and contact
rows; y' free on room rows and anti-peak rows; kappa free on the d-rows) with
 -e_p = sum_l y_l e_l + sum_{contacts} y_j z_j L_j + sum_{rooms} y'_j L_j + sum_{anti} y'_l e_l + sum_m kappa_m Q_m
(as linear functionals on R^{B_*}), and for EVERY tau in R^{B_*}:
 (1 + y_p) tau_p <= ||(y, y', kappa)||_inf [ sum_{l != p}(tau_l)_- + sum_{contacts}(z_j L_j(tau))_- + sum_{rooms}|L_j(tau)| +
                     sum_{anti}|tau_l| + sum_m |Q_m(tau)| ].
Proof.  -nu_p >= 0 on the polyhedral cone C_0^+ (rigidity and nu_p >= 0), so by Farkas' lemma -e_p lies in the dual cone, which is
generated by the inequality rows with nonnegative and the equality rows with free coefficients.  Evaluate at tau, move y_p tau_p to
the left, and bound each remaining term from below by minus its negative part (inequality rows) or its absolute value.  QED
K_A(l_*) := max over swallowing-type peaks p <= l_* d-rigid at level l_* of the least ||(y,y',kappa)||_inf (finite: LP).

Use in the window argument.  For a two-sided decomposition at t in W(l_*) (steps (1)-(2) of Theorem U, or Z4 Steps 1-4):
(tau_l)_- <= c/(2 m_l); contact and room violations <= c/2, c/gamma_T (c = c(tau) <= C_f(1 + K_*) t); anti-sign peaks |tau| <=
lambda K_d t + c/(2m) (peakshift + budget); |Q_m(tau)| = |delta_m| <= |Delta d_m| M_m + 2t/sigma_m + K_* t/(mC_m) + 6t^5/(mC_m)
(didentity: the coarse bad carriers contribute -Q_m(tau), good and fine ones are O(K_* t)).  Hence every d-rigid swallowing-type
peak satisfies |tau_p| <= (1 + K_A(l_*)) C_f (1 + K_* + K_d)(1 + 1/gamma_T)(1 + sum_{l <= l_*} 1/m_l) t: it is dropped with the
f-rate K_A in place of the margin rate 1/mu_p, and NO margin (in particular no non-degeneracy) is needed for it.

THEOREM V (d-rigid peaks need no margins) [PROVED by inserting Proposition P into Theorem U step (3), resp. into Z4 Theorem A /
Corollary 4.1 at the bound of the peak rows (their drop-row violation |tau_p|); referee check recommended].
(a) In Theorem U, (E4)'s exclusion of degenerate swallowing-type peaks in compensated blocks may be replaced by: every such peak is
d-rigid at all levels; and K_P(l) need only run over the NON-rigid swallowing-type peaks of compensated blocks; (W_U) acquires the
factor (1 + K_A(l)) inside the bracket.
(b) In Z4 Theorem A (design SLD_G) and Z4 Corollary 4.1 (original design, f-dependent Hoffman constants), hypothesis (H3) may be
replaced by (H3_rig): every degenerate swallowing-sign swallowed peak is d-rigid at all levels; and M_f(l) may be replaced by the sum
of the reciprocal margins of the non-rigid swallowing-sign swallowed peaks, plus K_A(l) (one more power of the growth bracket).
Reading.  In an uncompensated block (5.3) every swallowing-type peak is d-rigid with the certificate y = q/q_p on the sign rows of the
same-sign set (2.4 is the special case), which is why Theorem U needed no margins there.  A swallowing-type peak that is NOT
d-rigid can be switched by a zero-cost, exactly d-neutral direction: an EXACT resource that the window two-piece data cannot carry
(they vanish at bad peaks).  This is the residual (H3)-type case.

Comparison with Z4 (read after Theorem U was proved; see Section 11).  Z4 Theorem A (SLD_G, configuration Hoffman constants G*(l),
(H2'), (H3), (DR), growth (W_inf) with the margin sum M_f, the gap rate gamma_f and gamma_T) contains the COMPENSATED part of Theorem U:
Z4's (DR) (fixed zero-cost repair directions with d-sums of both signs) generalizes the resonant compensator pair, and Z4 Step 7 makes
the gap of kept q < 0 carriers a rate (Lemma onesidedtransfer: c_flat = min(c^0, gamma/(2A_2)), t_1 independent of gamma; Theorem
windowed works with a j-dependent c_flat).  Z4 Corollary 4.1 contains the uncompensated case at the level of f-dependent Hoffman
constants.  What Theorem U adds is the margin-free and design-constant treatment of uncompensated blocks (weak and degenerate
swallowing-type peaks; near-threshold d-coupled non-peaks), and Theorem V generalizes it to d-rigid peaks in any block.
