## 3.1 Theorem 3.1 (D^{U1} admissible, N-free, tools survive).  Verdict: the IDEA is sound, the DEFINITION is incomplete and in one point
## inconsistent; with the corrected design D^{U1'} below the statement is PROVED.
Defects found:
(D1) "Main stage" is never defined, and the "adaptive" ladder bijection is not specified.  Under the literal reading "every stage at which
     T_final's main/FD target is placed is followed by a cluster" the clusters (length 2|E^ch(L)| >= L) make main stages exponentially sparse,
     and the rule (A3) "serve the oldest pending deep request when one is due" does not guarantee the deadline psi^{-1}(s) of Lemma 3.2's proof
     (if "due" = released, deep pairs consume all stages and main stages starve; if "due" = near the deadline, a long cluster can contain
     the whole interval [s, psi^{-1}(s))).  Lemma 3.2 needs exactly: no main target meets s after L before an absorber pair for s.
(D2) INCONSISTENCY with (W6).  T_final's (W6) (V4's (GM)) imposes c_l <= g_l 2^{m+k-l} with g_l = delta^max_l H_l/(32 * 3^{|supp y_l|}).  For an
     absorber target |supp y| = R + 2 and R is the least integer with 2^{3-R} <= mu_{p_0}^2 c_p^2/4; inserting c_p <= K 3^{-R-2} gives
     8 (9/2)^R <= mu^2 K^2/324 < 1 — impossible for every R.  So absorber targets cannot satisfy (A0) and (W6) simultaneously; at cluster
     stages U1 even defines c_{L+1} as a minimum including (W6) for a target whose R depends on c_{L+1} (circular).
(D3) At cluster stages (W2), (W5), (W7) of T_final are violated by c_{L+i} = c_{L+1} 4^{1-i} (ratio 1/4 instead of super-small).  (W2) and
     the sub-window data are needed after the cluster (see (D4)); (W5), (W7) are V4's (SF*), (SF_tau): they are not used on U1's dependency
     tree, but D^{U1} then does NOT carry V4's results (Prop. 5.3, Theorem 5.6) — U1's claim "every refereed tool survives" must be restricted.
(D4) The proof of Lemma 4.2(ii) uses c_{k+1} <= b(k, M(k))^2 for every owner k; inside a cluster c_{k+1} = c_k/4.  The conclusion survives
     (part 4) only if sub-window data (step (6) of T_final) are defined at EVERY stage and (W2) holds at every non-cluster stage.
CORRECTED DESIGN D^{U1'} (PROVED admissible and N-free; proof as U1's Theorem 3.1 plus the remarks below).
 * Stages are produced in ROUNDS r = 1, 2, ...: let m_r run through the blocks in a fixed sequence containing every block infinitely often,
   and let y be T_final's candidate target for block m_r (allowed by (a), (c) at the stage where it will be placed).
   PRE: for every s in supp y ∩ (union_{l'} S_{l'}) place, at consecutive stages of block 1, a biased absorber pair for (s, +1), (s, -1);
   MAIN: place y at the next stage (block m_r);  CLUSTER: then one biased pair (block 1) for every s in E^ch(L) (L the main stage).
   Rule (c) is applied with the margin "supp y ∩ S_{l'} ⊂ [1, L - 2|supp y| - 1]" so that the pre-pairs themselves satisfy (c).  The deep pairs,
   psi and rule (c'') are not needed.  The ladder bijection is the one generated by this schedule (every block gets infinitely many stages).
 * Weights: T_final's (W1)-(W5), (W7) with (W4'') at PRE and MAIN stages, WITHOUT (W6) at absorber stages (absorber targets are chosen with
   R defined from the lower bound c_p^low := min{c_{p-1}/4, b(p-1, M(p-1))^2, (delta^max_p H_p/2)^2, (W4'')(s), (W5), (W7)}, which does not
   depend on R; the actual c_p >= c_p^low); at CLUSTER stages c_{L+i} := c_{L+1}^low 4^{1-i}.  Sub-window data (6) of T_final are defined at
   EVERY stage (the chain T_hi(w^+) <= T_lo(w) runs through all stages); windows are USED only at main stages.
 * Design(L) at main stages additionally contains 4^{R_cl(L)}, 2^{k(L+R_cl)}, and the counts D_cls(w), and the rate scheme contains the
   minors of Gamma^#(kappa, a) and the cofactor minors of part 2 (p-c), with their Lojasiewicz constants in C_L, N_L, Lip.
 Checks.  (T-a)-(T-d), (P1): as U1 (absorber, pre and cluster targets satisfy (a), (c); (W4) holds at all stages; every y^(i) is placed
 infinitely often in every block, no target is ever blocked).  (P2) at main stages: sum_{l'>L} c_{l'} <= (4/3) c_{L+1} + ... <= 2 b(L, M(L))^2.
 (W3) at cluster stages holds automatically: c_{L+i} <= b(L, M(L))^2 <= 2^{-8 n(L,M(L))} while (delta_{L+i} H_{L+i})^2 >= 2^{-2(L+i) - 6 * 2^{L+i} - 4}
 and L + i <= 3L + 2 s_max(L) << n(L, M(L)) (Design(L) >= 2^{6 s_max(L)}).  N-freeness: absorbers are in block 1, present in every p_N.
 LEMMA 3.2 under D^{U1'} (PROVED).  For a main stage L and s in E_c(w): if s in E^ch(L) the cluster of L follows L; otherwise s in S_{l''},
 l'' <= L, s > L, and every carrier after L meeting s is an absorber pair for s (pre-pair or cluster pair: their targets meet only their
 own s and fresh odd coordinates), a main target containing s (preceded by its pre-pair for s), or nothing (FD targets and signature vectors
 of other carriers avoid s).  Hence the first two carriers after L meeting s (if any) are a consecutive biased pair for s.  If no carrier
 after L meets s, there is no residue at s and nothing has to be absorbed.

## 3.3 Proposition 3.4 (exact absorption on E_c).  Verdict: CORRECT (PROVED) given parts 2 and 4, with precisions.
(a) the residue at s comes from carriers after the pair (the partner belongs to the pair; U1's "c_{p+1}" should read "stages >= p+2");
(b) exact cancellation: correct for any real residue; (c) capacity: correct — the pair's weight dominates every post-pair contribution at s
because the next non-cluster stage after a cluster (or a pre-pair block) has weight <= b(previous stage)^2, astronomically below
lambda_a T_lo(w) eta_a (Design of that stage contains B_mu >= 2^{2 p_0^2} and D >= 1/Phi); the trace corrections on T(L) are absorbed by the
cluster pairs of L (capacity >= A_2 c_{L+1}/(Design(L) t)); (d) no flips at the new support coordinates: t|b| <= C t gamma_a <= theta_a once
theta_a := C_f Design lambda_a T_hi; (e) value zero: no d-effect, nu_a = 0.
Remark (what absorption cannot do): only residues of size <= capacity ~ c_{L+1}/t can be absorbed; coarse values of size ~1/t on T(L) must be
admissible by themselves ((X1) stays in Gamma^#).  This is why the trace corrections must be O(c_{L+1}) and why kappa^# - kappa' must be tiny.
