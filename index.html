Functional Analysis · Banach Space Theory

Manwook Han 한만욱

I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics in August 2026 from Chungbuk National University, under the supervision of Sun Kwang Kim, and am currently exploring academic opportunities.

My research is in functional analysis, specifically the geometry of Banach spaces. I work on when operators and polynomials attain their norms, on the structure of spaces of compact operators, and on the shape of unit balls of spaces of homogeneous polynomials over polygonal domains.

Unit balls of the ℓ1, ℓ2 and ℓ∞ norms on the plane.

Research interests

  • Norm-attaining mappings. Bishop–Phelps–Bollobás type properties, weak maximizing and minimizing properties, and norm-attainment in projective tensor products.
  • M-ideals of compact operators. When the space of compact operators is an M-ideal in the space of bounded operators, and the consequences for norm attainment.
  • Geometry of Banach spaces and Banach lattices. Extreme and order extreme points, polyhedrality, and the interaction between order and norm.
  • Tensor products of Banach spaces. Projective and injective tensor products, nuclear and integral operators.
  • Polynomials and multilinear mappings on Banach spaces. Extremal problems for homogeneous polynomials, and unit balls of polynomial spaces over polygonal domains.
